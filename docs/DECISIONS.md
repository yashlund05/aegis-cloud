# Aegis Design Decisions Log & Split Protocol

This document enforces the **Split Protocol** governing experimental integrity, parameter selection, and architectural design choices.

---

## 1. Split Protocol Definition

To guarantee scientific validity, prevent p-hacking, and eliminate test-set leakage:
1. **Partition Roles:**
   - **Train Set (30 Apps):** Used exclusively for model parameter optimization and feature learning.
   - **Calibration Set (10 Apps):** Used exclusively for computing conformal inference non-conformity scores and margin offsets.
   - **Validation Set (20 Apps — Formerly Designated "Test Partition"):** Usable strictly for design decisions, grid sweeps, threshold selection, and algorithm ablation.
   - **Expanded-Test Set (Future W4):** Strict zero-shot blind evaluation. **Under no circumstances may any app from the future expanded-test set be inspected, swept, or used to tune hyperparameters, control arms, or thresholds.**
2. **Audit Requirement:**
   - Every architectural modification, hyperparameter selection, grid choice, and safety guard fix must be justified by evidence from the Calibration and Validation partitions only.
   - Every decision must be logged below with: **Date Written**, **Commit Hash**, **Rationale**, and **All Candidates Tried (including failed arms)**.
   - Any historical decisions documented post-hoc must be explicitly tagged as `RETROSPECTIVE`. Do not backdate any entries.

### Decision Entry D-6: Shortfall Target Hierarchy (Primary 1.0%, Secondary 0.1%, Supplementary 0% & 5%)
- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Commit:** `3176fbf5ca747b82c760456ee471aaa4a6876292`
- **Status:** **ACTIVE DECISION RECORD**
- **Context:** Task W2b extrapolation audit and target hierarchy classification. Following the expansion to widened grids in Task W2 (`eval/scale_aware_pareto_results_v3.json`), extrapolation rates vary substantially across target shortfall percentages ($0.0\%$, $0.1\%$, $1.0\%$, $5.0\%$). To prevent misinterpretation of boundary-clamped artifacts, a formal hierarchy of targets is established based strictly on validation-set extrapolation evidence.
- **Target Hierarchy & Validation Evidence:**
  1. **Primary Target — 1.0% Shortfall (187.2 min / 18,720 min):**
     - *Status:* **PRIMARY BENCHMARK**
     - *Justification:* Demonstrates the lowest extrapolation rates across all evaluated autoscaling configurations. On the 20 validation apps, CA requires extrapolation for only $6/20$ apps ($30\%$), Scale-Aware Aegis for only $3/20$ apps ($15\%$), Rolling for $6/20$ ($30\%$), and ACI ($\gamma=0.020$) for $5/20$ ($25\%$). All non-extrapolated cohorts have $N \ge 10$ apps, providing statistically powerful paired difference estimation without distortion from frontier edge clamping.
  2. **Secondary Target — 0.1% Shortfall (18.72 min / 18,720 min):**
     - *Status:* **SECONDARY BENCHMARK (Strict SLA)**
     - *Justification:* Represents a stringent service-level target ($99.9\%$ capacity adherence). Scale-Aware Aegis achieves minimal extrapolation ($3/20$, $15\%$), and CA requires extrapolation for $7/20$ ($35\%$). Valid cohorts ($N \ge 9$) confirm statistically significant energy savings for Scale-Aware ($-71.19\text{ kWh}$, 95% CI $[-162.94, -0.72]$) and Rolling ($-92.72\text{ kWh}$, 95% CI $[-203.13, -8.78]$). However, ACI $\gamma=0.005$ is underpowered ($N=6/20$).
  3. **Supplementary Target — 0.0% Shortfall (0.0 min):**
     - *Status:* **SUPPLEMENTARY (Zero Shortfall Lower Bound)**
     - *Justification:* Useful for characterizing extreme conservative over-provisioning. However, $100\%$ of remaining extrapolations ($10/20$ for CA, $7/20$ for Scale-Aware, $13/20$ for Rolling, $18/20$ for ACI) are strictly `below_min_shortfall`. Hypothesis: Certain bursty workloads incur unavoidable node boot latency spikes ($>0\text{ min}$) even at maximum over-provisioning ($U=0.10$, $\tau=0.999$), causing the interpolation algorithm to clamp at the frontier edge.
  4. **Supplementary Target — 5.0% Shortfall (936.0 min):**
     - *Status:* **SUPPLEMENTARY (High Shortfall Upper Bound)**
     - *Justification:* Useful as an upper bracket for relaxed SLOs. However, extrapolations are overwhelmingly `above_max_shortfall` ($7/8$ for CA, $8/8$ for Scale-Aware, $11/11$ for Rolling/ACI) Hypothesis: Autoscaler stabilization windows and minimum instance guarantees prevent total shortfall from reaching 936 minutes on low-load workloads.
- **Split Protocol Compliance:**
  - This target prioritization was established solely from analyzing the extrapolation counts and frontier ranges of the 20 validation applications in `eval/scale_aware_pareto_results_v3.json` and `eval/extrapolation_sensitivity.json`. Zero expanded-test set (W4) data was inspected.

### Decision Entry D-11: Baselines and Ablations Experimental Protocol (Task W3)
- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Commit:** To be committed prior to running baselines study (`eval/baselines_results_v1.json`)
- **Status:** **ACTIVE PROTOCOL SPECIFICATION**
- **Context:** Task W3 evaluation of alternative forecasters and system component ablations through the frozen v5 simulation pipeline.
- **Experimental Design & Arms:**
  1. **Uniform Conformal Wrapping:**
     - To isolate pure forecaster quality from downstream calibration mechanics, every baseline forecaster outputs a causal point forecast $\hat{y}(t)$ and is wrapped with the EXACT SAME causal finite-sample rolling conformal wrapper ($W=1440$, $H=10$, level $=\min(1.0, \lceil (n+1)\tau \rceil / n)$) over the identical $\tau$ grid ($\tau \in \{0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60, 0.70, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, 0.95, 0.975, 0.99, 0.995, 0.999\}$).
     - The upper bound stream is $p_{90}^{\text{conf}}(t) = \hat{y}(t) + q_{\tau}(t)$, exactly mirroring the primary Aegis arm.
  2. **Baseline Forecasters:**
     - `seasonal_naive`: Lagged actual demand at $t - 1440$ (24 hours prior) ported from `eval/run_real_pareto.py`.
     - `holt_winters`: `statsmodels.tsa.holtwinters.ExponentialSmoothing` with additive trend and additive daily seasonality (144 10-minute periods), fit strictly on data preceding the scored test day, refit at most once per 24 hours on 10-minute downsampled averages, and upsampled to 1-minute steps. Runtime estimated at $\sim 1.8\text{ s}$ per app ($\sim 36\text{ s}$ total across 20 validation apps).
     - `quantile_linear`: Standard linear quantile regression model (`sklearn.linear_model.QuantileRegressor`) trained on the identical 45 features extracted from the 30 TRAIN apps exclusively (zero validation or test app leakage).
     - `lightgbm_point_rolling`: Control arm passing the raw LightGBM point forecast (p50) through the same point+rolling wrapper to prove the wrapper is not the cause of any observed difference.
  3. **Component Ablations:**
     - `fixed_margin`: Raw LightGBM p90 forecast plus a constant additive headroom margin swept over grid $[0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0, 8.6468]$ cores (forming a matched frontier across margin values).
     - `no_cpsat_ffd`: The rolling-conformal Aegis arm where the joint optimization placement is bypassed and replaced with the First-Fit-Decreasing (FFD) consolidation fallback (`has_placement_opt=False`, uniform/unoptimized spreading across active nodes).
  4. **Simulation & Metrics Invariants:**
     - All arms are evaluated through the exact same frozen simulation logic (`AblationStudy._simulate_configuration`), cluster topology (20 large nodes, 68 allocatable cores), safety guards ($10\%$ dead zone, 300s cooldown, 3-minute node boot latency, HPA scale step bounds), and energy accounting ($P_{\text{idle}}=100\text{ W}$, $P_{\text{max}}=300\text{ W}$, $\alpha=1.5$).

     - Direct paired delta vs primary Aegis rolling arm is computed with convention $\Delta E = E_{\text{Aegis}} - E_{\text{baseline}}$ (negative indicates Aegis is cheaper).
